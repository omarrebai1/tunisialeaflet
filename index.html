Blue horizons

Tunisia opens onto the Mediterranean Sea, with turquoise water, sandy beaches, and sunny coastal towns. From Hammamet and Sousse to Djerba, visitors can swim, sail, dive, or simply enjoy the seaside.

🏜️

Sahara magic

Discover dramatic dunes, oasis towns, and unforgettable desert sunsets in the south. Desert excursions, camel rides, and traditional villages offer a memorable adventure.

Tourism in Tunisia

A destination for every kind of traveler

Tourism is an important part of Tunisia’s economy. Travelers come for its beaches and resorts, but also for its rich heritage, lively souks, ancient ruins, desert landscapes, and welcoming culture. Whether you want relaxation, history, or adventure, Tunisia offers it all in one journey.
